Financial Sentiment Classification with BERT Fine-Tuning

Problem
General sentiment models can struggle with financial language, while class imbalance obscures errors on positive and negative news.
Approach
Compared bag-of-words baselines, pretrained BERT, head-only adaptation and full fine-tuning on a 2,423-sentence sample with separate training, validation and test splits.
Impact
Full fine-tuning achieved reported test accuracy of 87% and macro F1 of 0.84, versus 76% and 0.64 for logistic regression. External FinBERT results were treated separately due to possible corpus overlap.
